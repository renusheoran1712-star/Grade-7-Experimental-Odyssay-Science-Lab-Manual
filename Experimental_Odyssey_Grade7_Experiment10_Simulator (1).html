CORE CONCEPT

Electric current has magnetic and heating effects.

The heating effect warms a wire. The magnetic effect creates a magnetic field around a current-carrying wire. A coil of insulated wire around a soft iron nail makes an electromagnet that can be switched on and off.

Aim: To see the magnetic effect of current, build an electromagnet and feel the heating effect.
