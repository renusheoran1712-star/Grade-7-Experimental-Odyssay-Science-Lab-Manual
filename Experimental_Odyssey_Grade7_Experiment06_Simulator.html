06 · Respiration in Organisms

CO₂ in breath & a model of breathing

Interactive virtual lab
🫁

🎯 Aim & core concept

To show that exhaled air contains carbon dioxide and to build a diaphragm model of breathing. Respiration releases energy from food inside cells; breathing moves air into and out of the lungs.

🧪 Part A · Limewater test for carbon dioxide

Manual procedure: put 5 mL fresh limewater in each of two test tubes. Keep one aside. Blow gently through a straw into the other for 1–2 minutes.

🫁 Part B · Diaphragm model of breathing

A Y-shaped tube connects two balloons inside a bell jar or cut plastic bottle. A rubber sheet covers the open bottom and represents the diaphragm.

Resting position
Balloon lungs · rubber-sheet diaphragm
Choose an action. Observe how the balloons change size when the rubber sheet moves.

📘 Observations & scientific explanation

Limewater · breath test
Blow through straw → clear limewater turns milky.
Exhaled air contains carbon dioxide.
Model · pull sheet down
Space grows; pressure falls; balloons inflate.
Models inhalation.
Model · push sheet up
Space shrinks; pressure rises; balloons shrink.
Models exhalation.
Scientific conclusion: Respiration releases CO₂. We breathe in when the diaphragm moves down and the ribs move up and out; we breathe out when they relax.
