08 · Reproduction in Plants

Explore flower parts, pollen, ovules and the formation of bread mould spores.

🎧 Listen & learn

Plants reproduce by seeds (sexual) or without seeds (asexual): cuttings, buds, tubers, leaf buds and spores. A flower has sepals, petals, stamens (male: anther and filament) and a pistil (female: stigma, style and ovary). After fertilisation the ovary becomes a fruit and the ovules become seeds.

🍞 Part B · Bread mould & spores

Place a few drops of water on bread, seal it in a plastic bag and keep it in a warm, dark place for 4 days. Observe the mould through the closed bag.

Stigma, style, ovary with ovules.
Makes seeds (female).
Bread mould
White fuzz, then black dots.
Black dots are sporangia full of spores.
