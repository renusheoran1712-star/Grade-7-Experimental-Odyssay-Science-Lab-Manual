05 · Physical & Chemical Changes

Explore two chemical changes: burning magnesium and iron displacing copper from copper sulphate.

🎧 Listen & learn

A physical change forms no new substance. A chemical change forms a new substance with new properties. In this experiment, magnesium burns in oxygen to form magnesium oxide, and iron displaces copper from copper sulphate solution.

🔥 Part A · Burning magnesium ribbon

Virtual steps follow the manual: clean a 3 cm Mg ribbon, ignite with tongs above a watch glass, collect white ash, mix it with 5 mL water, and test with litmus.
