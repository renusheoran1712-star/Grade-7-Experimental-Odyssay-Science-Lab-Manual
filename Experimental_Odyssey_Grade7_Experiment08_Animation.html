Flower reproduction and spores

1. Flower reproduction

A flower is the reproductive part of a plant. It has sepals, petals, stamens and a pistil.

After fertilisation the ovary becomes a fruit and the ovules become seeds.

🧠 Key learning

Stamen
Anther with yellow pollen on a filament.
Makes pollen (male).
Pistil
Stigma, style, ovary with ovules.
Makes seeds (female).
Bread mould
White fuzz, then black dots.
Black dots are sporangia full of spores.
⚠️ Safety first: Never open the mould bag or breathe in spores; hand it to the teacher to throw away sealed. Only the teacher uses the blade. Wash your hands afterwards.
