🎧 Listen & learn

Acids taste sour and turn blue litmus red. Bases feel soapy and turn red litmus blue. Indicators such as litmus, turmeric and red cabbage juice change colour in acids and bases.

📘 Observation guide

Lemon juice
Blue litmus turns red; nature: acid.
Soap solution
Red litmus turns blue; turmeric turns red-brown; nature: base.
Tap water
Litmus shows no change; nature: neutral.
After neutralising
Litmus shows no change; the manual records 28 → 31 °C.

The manual's observation table also records: vinegar is acidic; baking-soda solution is basic. Your own practical observations may be entered in your notebook.

⚠️ Safety first: This is a virtual model, not a replacement for the practical. Dilute HCl and NaOH can irritate skin and eyes and must be teacher-prepared. Wear goggles and gloves, rinse spills with plenty of water, and never taste any laboratory liquid. Follow your teacher's instructions.
