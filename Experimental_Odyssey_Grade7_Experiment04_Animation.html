1. Meet the indicators

Indicators are substances that change colour in acidic and basic solutions. We can use them to test everyday liquids safely.

Key idea: indicators help us identify whether a liquid is acidic, basic or neutral.

🔎 Remember these indicator clues

Acids
Blue litmus turns red. Turmeric stays yellow.
Bases
Red litmus turns blue. Turmeric turns red-brown.
Neutral liquids
Litmus shows no change. Turmeric stays yellow.
Red cabbage juice
Acid → pink/red; neutral → purple; base → blue/green.
⚠️ Safety first: Never taste laboratory liquids. Dilute HCl and NaOH must be teacher-prepared and handled with goggles and gloves. Rinse spills with plenty of water and tell your teacher. This animation is a learning model, not a replacement for supervised practical work.
