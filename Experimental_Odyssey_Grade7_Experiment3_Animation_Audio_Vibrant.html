Scene 1 · Set up a fair test

Place two similar cans at the same distance from the heat lamp. Put a thermometer in each can and start with equal temperatures.

Key idea: change the surface, but keep the other conditions the same.

The temperatures shown are illustrative model values, not guaranteed practical results. Record your own observed readings during the real experiment.

🎯 Aim

To compare heat absorption by black and shiny surfaces.

🧪 Apparatus

Two similar cans, thermometers and a heat lamp.

👀 Expected observation

The black can generally warms faster than the reflective can under the same conditions.

💡 Conclusion

Dark matt surfaces absorb more radiation; shiny surfaces reflect more radiation.
Safety first: The lamp and cans can become hot. Do not touch hot equipment. Keep water away from electrical equipment and perform the practical under teacher/adult supervision.
