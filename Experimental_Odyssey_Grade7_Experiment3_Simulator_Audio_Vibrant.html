🔬 Observation and conclusion

Observation
The black can generally warms faster than the shiny can when both receive radiation under comparable conditions.
Why?
Dark matt surfaces absorb more incoming radiation; shiny reflective surfaces reflect more.
Fair test
Keep can size, starting temperature, lamp distance and heating time the same.
