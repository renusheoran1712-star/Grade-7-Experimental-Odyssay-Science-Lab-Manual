SCENE 1 OF 4

1. Clean the magnesium ribbon

Rub a 3 cm magnesium ribbon with sandpaper to remove its dull surface layer. It should look shiny and silvery.

Cleaning exposes fresh magnesium so it can react readily with oxygen.

🧭 Experiment pathway

🧽1 · Clean Mg

Use sandpaper on a 3 cm ribbon.

🔥2 · Burn safely

Teacher demonstrates with tongs; collect white ash.

🧪3 · Test ash

Mix with 5 mL water; test with red and blue litmus.

🔩4 · Iron in CuSO₄

Add a clean iron nail and wait 30 minutes.

⚠️ Safety first: Teacher supervision is essential. Never look directly at burning magnesium—the dazzling white glare can hurt your eyes. Use tongs, keep flammable items away, and do not touch or taste copper sulphate solution.
