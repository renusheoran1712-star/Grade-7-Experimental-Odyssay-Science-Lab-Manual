🌱 Experiment 12 · Soil

Interactive virtual lab: compare water percolation through sandy, clayey and loamy soil, then separate soil layers by sedimentation.

🔊 Audio-enabled virtual lab

🎧 Listen & Learn

Soil forms over thousands of years as rocks weather and mix with humus. Sandy soil has large grains and drains fast. Clayey soil has tiny grains and holds water. Loam is a balanced mix and is best for crops.

💧 Part A · Water Percolation

Manual setup: pack 15 cm of each soil in separate pipes, pour 200 mL water, time the draining and calculate rate = 200 mL ÷ time in minutes.

Jar
After 6 hours
Gravel, sand, silt and clay settle in order; humus floats.
Scientific conclusion: Sandy soil drains fastest, clay slowest and loam in between. In water, soil settles in layers by grain size: the heaviest first.
