Step-by-Step Photosynthesis Journey

🌱
Step 1 of 5

1. Destarching the Plant

The potted plant is placed in a dark room for 48 to 72 hours. Without light, photosynthesis stops and the plant consumes all existing starch stored in its leaves.

Key Concepts & Reactions

  • Photosynthesis Equation: Carbon dioxide + Water $\xrightarrow{\text{Sunlight, Chlorophyll}}$ Glucose + Oxygen.
  • Stomata: Microscopic pores located mainly on the lower epidermis of leaves, flanked by two bean-shaped guard cells.
  • Role of KOH: Potassium hydroxide chemically reacts with carbon dioxide ($KOH + CO_2 \rightarrow K_2CO_3 + H_2O$), making it unavailable inside the sealed bottle.
  • Iodine Test: Iodine solution reacts with starch to produce a dark blue-black complex. Unreacted areas stay brown-yellow.

Safety First (Golden Rules)

  • KOH Handling: KOH is a strong corrosive alkali. Wear safety goggles and gloves. Teacher supervision required.
  • Alcohol Flammability: Never heat ethanol/alcohol directly over a open Bunsen flame! Always use a boiling water bath.
  • Leaf Handling: Handle delicate leaves with forceps to prevent breaking during decolorization.

Understanding Pendulum Oscillations

⏱️
Step 1 of 4

1. Mean Position vs Extreme Position

When the pendulum bob rests vertically at rest, it is at its Mean Position (O). Displacing it to A releases potential energy, causing it to swing back and forth.

Key Formulae & Definitions

  • One Oscillation: Movement of the bob from mean position O to extreme A, then to extreme B, and back to O ($O \rightarrow A \rightarrow B \rightarrow O$).
  • Time Period ($T$): Time taken by the pendulum to complete one full oscillation ($T = \frac{\text{Total Time}}{\text{Number of Oscillations}}$).
  • Formula: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$, where $L$ is thread length and $g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$. Time period increases as length $L$ increases.
  • Uniform Motion: Object moving along a straight line with constant speed produces a straight line Distance-Time graph.

Lab Precautions

  • Small Displacement: The displacement angle should be small (< 15°) to ensure true simple harmonic motion.
  • No Spinning: Release the metallic bob gently without pushing or spinning it.
  • Avoid Drafts: Ensure fans are turned off so air currents do not damp the oscillation timing.
