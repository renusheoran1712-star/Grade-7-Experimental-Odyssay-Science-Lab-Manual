🎧 Listen & Learn

Light travels in straight lines. Refraction bends light when it passes between transparent materials. A glass prism disperses white light into seven colours, and a convex lens brings parallel rays together at the focus.

🌈 Part A · Dispersion of White Light

Follow the manual: shine a narrow torch beam onto one face of a glass prism, turn the prism slowly, and observe the spectrum on the white screen.

Stage
Observed order
Violet → Indigo → Blue → Green → Yellow → Orange → Red
Shine the narrow beam onto the prism.

🔍 Part B · Convex Lens Focus

Face the convex lens toward a distant window or tree. Move the white card until the sharpest small image appears. Measure the distance from the lens to the card three times.

Stage
Manual observations
14.8 cm, 15.2 cm, 15.0 cm
Mean focal length = 15.0 cm
Face the convex lens toward a distant window or tree.

📊 Observation Table

Prism
A band of violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange and red.
Dispersion: violet bends most; red least.
Lens and window
A sharp, small, upside-down image on the card.
Far-away rays are almost parallel and meet at the focus.
Focal length
14.8 cm, 15.2 cm, 15.0 cm
