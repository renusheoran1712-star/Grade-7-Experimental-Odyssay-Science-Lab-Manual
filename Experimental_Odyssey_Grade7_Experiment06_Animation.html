1. What is respiration?

Respiration is the process in cells that breaks down food (glucose) to release energy. Breathing is the movement of air into and out of the lungs.

Breathing and respiration are related, but they are not the same process.

🧠 What the model teaches

Balloon lungs
Show the lungs expanding and shrinking.
Rubber-sheet diaphragm
Pull down: balloons inflate. Push up: balloons deflate.
Limewater test
Exhaled air contains carbon dioxide; limewater turns milky.
⚠️ Safety first: Blow gently through a straw; never suck limewater. Keep limewater away from eyes and wash hands afterwards. A plastic bottle works if no bell jar is available. Teacher supervision is recommended.
